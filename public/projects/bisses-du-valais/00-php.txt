Project Title : Bisses du Valais

Project Description :
This project explores a contemporary redesign of the Bisses du Valais website through a Figma-based prototype. I developed a navigation concept based on fluid lines running across the interface, inspired by water flow and combined with an interactive map and a dedicated museum section. These lines structure scrolling, orientation, and access to territorial information throughout the site.
