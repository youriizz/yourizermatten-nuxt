The project positions the website not as a neutral communication tool, but as an experiential space in itself — a place where the event happens rather than merely being announced.
The name “randomshit666.com” reflects a critical stance toward the homogenization of the web: “random” embraces chaos and unpredictability as a creative method; “shit” rejects polished perfection in favor of the rough, the discarded, and the unresolved; and “666” subverts the profane to question the sacralization of smooth, standardized digital aesthetics.
