GRAUmagazin is a Berlin-based art magazine. I designed and developed its website using Kirby CMS, balancing a strong visual identity with usability — rethinking UI patterns for long-form content and evolving editorial needs. The site features two homepages: a classic vertical-scroll view and an alternative map-based layout activated with "change views."
